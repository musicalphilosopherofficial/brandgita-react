About Brand Gita

Brand Gita helps entrepreneurs, coaches and educators turn their long-form content into 30 days of on-brand posts — in their own voice, not a template’s.

Who builds it

Brand Gita is built by Utsav Trivedi — composer of Brand Gita, YouTuber and founder of Karma OS — based in Lower Hutt, New Zealand. Brand Gita is operated by Utsav Dhruv Trivedi, trading as Brand Gita.

Why it exists

Creators who do long-form work are exhausted by the production cycle: editing, repurposing, hiring editors who don’t quite get their style, and AI tools that make everyone look the same. Brand Gita captures your brand once, then makes every post look and sound like you.
