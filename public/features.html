Features & integrations

Brand Gita turns your long-form content into on-brand posts and publishes them to your own accounts — only when you review and confirm each post.

Brand interview

You answer questions about your voice, style and audience once. Your brand is kept on file, and every carousel, caption and video that follows inherits it — no re-briefing, ever. Style profiles lock in your look so every new post follows it.

Carousels

On-brand slides built from your long-form content, your expertise (education carousels supported by diagrams) and your own photos and videos from your life, turned into personal story carousels. Captions are written in your voice. A content bank keeps every idea in one place, and your content plans are made from it.

Finished-video publishing

Finished videos — edited and styled to your brand — are published straight to your own accounts. Videos upload from your own device. You review the post and press Post; nothing is scheduled or sent on your behalf without that confirmation.

Direct publishing

Brand Gita publishes to your own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts, with no third-party scheduler in between. You connect each account yourself and can disconnect it at any time.

Publishing to TikTok

Brand Gita uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API so you can post to your own TikTok account. This is how it works:

  1. You connect your own TikTok account. Brand Gita shows your TikTok account name and the posting options your account allows.
  2. You choose who can see the post. There is no default — you must pick a privacy option for every post.
  3. Comments, Duet and Stitch are off by default. You switch on only what you want, and only the options your account allows.
  4. You say whether the post promotes a brand (your own business or a paid partnership), and the post is labelled accordingly.
  5. Nothing is sent until you review and press Post. Brand Gita never posts automatically on your behalf.
  6. Videos upload from your device. Photo carousels are pulled by TikTok from images hosted at assets.brandgita.com for the time it takes to publish, then removed.
  7. Send to drafts. You can instead send a video to your TikTok drafts and finish the post inside TikTok.
  8. No watermark is added to your content.
  9. Disconnecting revokes access. When you disconnect TikTok, Brand Gita's access to your account is revoked. See Data deletion for how to remove your data.

Brand Gita is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok. See our Privacy Policy and Terms for details on how TikTok data is handled.
